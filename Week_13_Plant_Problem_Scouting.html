Plant Problem Scouting
and Documentation

Look closely. Record evidence. Explain your thinking.

Your field investigation

  1. Visit every station assigned by your instructor. Start anywhere within the designated boundaries.
  2. Take setting, whole-plant, and close-up photographs. Leave flags and plants undisturbed.
  3. Record what you see, then explain what you suspect and what you would check next.
  4. After the class walkthrough, revise your thinking and complete the reflection.

Submit your report in Canvas. This form does not send your work to your instructor. Use Save report as PDF below, then upload that PDF to the lab assignment.

Drafts and photos are stored in this browser on this device when supported. Keep using the same browser. Download a backup before closing, clearing browser data, or switching devices. There is no live instructor dashboard.
